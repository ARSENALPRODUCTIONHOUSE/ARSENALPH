Why it works

Trust isn’t bought.
It’s built one week at a time.

Nobody buys off the first video. But when the same person sees you for the third week running, they’ve stopped sizing you up and started knowing you. A series is the machine that produces that repetition, without you paying for attention from scratch every time.

Anticipation is the new currency

An ordinary post fights for attention for a second and a half. People come asking for the next episode of a series. And a returning viewer is far more likely to buy than a random scroller.

The platforms push it

In 2026 TikTok and Meta rolled out dedicated series features and reward episodic content. The algorithm measures whether a viewer comes back, and a series gives it exactly that signal.

A character can’t be copied

Your competitor can copy your post by tomorrow. Your craftsman, your team and the inside jokes between you, those they can’t copy. Ever.

The episode warms the audience up. The offer collects the sale. Neither works half as well alone as they do together. SERIES + SALES WINDOWS

FAQ

Questions everyone asks

Our team doesn’t want to be on camera. What then?

A completely normal worry, and there are several answers. We bring our own actors, make a series where the story is carried by hands, products and the setting, or start with the Test package where we put the boldest person up front and see how it goes. Honestly, most teams loosen up in front of the camera by the second shoot day.

Are actors included in the price?

No, because we don’t know in advance whether they’re needed at all or how many. The number and level of actors depends entirely on the script and your budget. Some series need one person, others need five. We agree on it once the script is set and put the price as a separate line in the quote.

How long is the contract and what if it doesn’t work?

The minimum is one season, three months, because anything shorter can’t prove itself. Notice is one month and the contract can be paused for a month if needed. At the end of the season we go over the numbers together and you decide whether a second season happens.

Who comes up with what we even film?

We do. You don’t have to come with ideas, that’s our job. Before the first shoot we write the full concept, the characters and every episode. We need only one thing from you: let us into your backroom and tell us honestly what goes on there.

How much of our own time does it take?

One shoot day a month and about an hour of communication. Everything else is on us. On the shoot day we film the whole month’s episodes at once, so your people don’t have to be on camera every week.

We already make big ads. Why do we need a series too?

A big ad gives you a moment, a series gives you a relationship. One expensive video a year reaches many once, but holds no one. A series keeps your brand alive the eleven months a campaign isn’t running, and makes the next big ad land harder, because the audience already knows who you are.

Are the prices final?

The numbers on the page are approximate and give the right ballpark. The final price is set after the strategy call, once we know exactly what you want to do. After that the price is locked for the whole season and there are no extra charges.
